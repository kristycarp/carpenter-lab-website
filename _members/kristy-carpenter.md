Kristy Carpenter is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biomedical Informatics at the University of Colorado Anschutz. She obtained her PhD in Biomedical Informatics from Stanford University and her BS in Computer Science and Molecular Biology from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. Her research centers on the development and application of machine learning models to accelerate drug discovery and contribute to biomedical knowledge, particularly with respect to addiction medicine.
