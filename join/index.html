We are currently building our inaugural team! See our open positions below.

Postdoctoral Researcher

Scientists with an MD or a PhD (or nearing completion of one) who have a strong background in either 1) machine learning at the molecular/structural level for drug discovery-related applications; or 2) substance use disorder research (machine learning background preferred but not required) may contact Dr. Carpenter directly via email. Please use the subject line Postdoc Application - Carpenter Lab. Include your CV and a cover letter.

PhD Rotation Student

Current first-year PhD students at CU Anschutz who are in Computational Bioscience, Biomedical Sciences, Human Medical Genetics and Genomics, Pharmaceutical Sciences, or another program related to the lab’s research, and who would like to rotate in the lab, should reach out to Dr. Carpenter directly via email.

MS Student

Current MS students at the University of Colorado Anschutz, University of Colorado Denver, or University of Colorado Boulder should apply via Handshake (Posting #11702418). Strong background in machine learning and data science is preferred.

Research Staff

Scientists with a BS or MS who are interested in a full-time staff position at the entry/intermediate level should apply via CU Careers. Strong background in machine learning and data science is preferred.

Undergraduate Researcher

Current juniors and seniors at the University of Colorado Denver or University of Colorado Boulder with experience in machine learning (i.e. previous research, internship, or class project) should apply via Handshake (Posting #11920318).
